Good to know

Questions

What's the age limit?+

The festival is 18+ after 6 pm. Under-18s are welcome before 6 pm with a ticketed adult, and children under 4 enter free.

Can I re-enter the venue?+

No. Exteacy Live has no re-entry on any ticket, so once you leave Z Studio your pass can't be used again.

How do I get there?+

Exteacy Live is at Z Studio, Jaipur. Cab drop-off is at the main gate, and the exact location pin and parking details come with your ticket.

Is it cashless?+

Yes. Load your wristband at the gate or with UPI at any top-up counter. Unused balance is refunded within seven days.

How cold does it get?+

Late-December nights in Jaipur drop to around 8°C, so bring a warm layer. The VIP lounge has heaters and there's free chai at every bar until midnight.
